This folder holds BiRefNet (https://github.com/ZhengPeng7/BiRefNet), as exported by onnx-community/BiRefNet-ONNX
(half precision), with its large join and cut steps split into smaller ones so browsers can run it
(tools/cutout_model.py in the Dawa project). Used by the admin panel to remove photo backgrounds.
